Mackity

Airwindows console saturation as a Live 12 audio effect. Final design, drawn at 2x; positions are in body px at 100 %.

Drag a dial up or down, Shift for fine. Double-click to reset. Click Makeup to switch it. Turn Drive up to see the band widen and Clip light.

Final design

Type 1 · 131 × 169 px body · Max openrect 127 wide

  • Display at (4, 4), 123 × 109: panel, live_lcd_bg, 2 px radius.
  • Curve at (8, 8), 100 × 84: v8ui. Border, crosshair, teal band for the driven range, curve 1.5 px, clipped inside the border. 4 px from the meter.
  • Meter at (112, 8), 11 × 84: live.meter~, stereo, Output level.
  • Clip light centred at (14.5, 102.5), r 2.5, then "Clip" at x 22, as in Saturator. Saturator's teal, fading up from all but invisible (led-off) to led-on; the label stays dim.
  • Makeup at (70, 96), 53 × 13: live.text toggle, LCD look, text padded 8 px a side like Saturator's Color. Outlined when off, orange when on. Default off.
  • Drive and Output: live.dial, ring centres at x 39.5 and 91.5 (52 apart), labels at cap 120.

Behaviour

  • Drive (In Trim, A): gain (10·A)², shown as 40 + 40·log₁₀A dB. Default 0.100 = 0.0 dB.
  • Output (Out Pad, B): gain B, shown as 20·log₁₀B dB. Default 1.000 = 0.0 dB.
  • Makeup on: Output gets an extra 1 / Drive gain, so the level stays put as Drive goes up.
  • Clip: brightness follows how far past Mackity's ±1 clamp the signal goes (dark at the clamp, full 6 dB past it). It lights at once and fades with a 0.3 s time constant, matching Saturator in Live. Exact clamp hits need a new outlet on aw.mackity~.
  • Band: the teal band shows the input range the signal covers right now. It follows the level with the same rise and fade, and is gone in silence.
  • Zoom: above 0 dB Drive the graph zooms out by the square root of the drive gain, as Saturator's does: each +6 dB pulls the clamp corner in by 3 dB and widens the band by 3 dB, so the band rarely pins. Measured against Saturator in Live.
