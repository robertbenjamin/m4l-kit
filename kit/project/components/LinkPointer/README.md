Links are drawn: a triangle ties a display region to the dial it edits.

## Spec (Channel EQ, 100 %)
- The display is shortened to end at 107 (103 tall) so the triangle and the notched dials fit: triangle 8 × 4 from y 106.5, labels at cap 115.5, notches under the labels, rings on the band.
- 8 × 4 triangle in `display`, hanging from the display's bottom edge, centred on the dial it points at (Channel EQ's Mid).
- For a toggle and its dial, use a 1 px connector instead (see Toggle).

## In Max
A `live.comment` glyph or a `panel` shape coloured from `themecolor.live_lcd_bg`.
