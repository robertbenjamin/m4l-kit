A checkbox turns a section or feature on, or a display layer on (a legend).

## Spec (100 %)
- Enabling a section in a display: 9 × 9, after its disclosure triangle and before the section name (Delay's and Echo's Filter).
- A legend in a display: 7 × 7 (Compressor's GR and Output).
- On is filled with the accent; off is a 1 px outline in the same accent.
- On the body, heading a section: 8 × 8, `orange` fill when on (Spectral Time's 1 Freezer, 2 Delay).
- When it turns a section off, every value in that section dims to `text-dim` (Chorus-Ensemble's 50.0 Hz, Saturator's Amt Lo).
- The checkbox comes first, then its name.

## When to use

- **Use** for enabling a section or feature; its values dim when off. Also legends in a display (Compressor GR / Output).
- **Don't** when switching a mode (text toggle or segmented).
- **Live 12 uses it** 14 times: Auto Shift, Compressor, Spectral Time, Roar, Chorus-Ensemble.
- **Max:** `live.toggle`, appearance 0 Default on the body, 1 LCD in a display; outputmode 1 Mouse up. Status: docs.
