A segmented pair is two short options side by side, often a unit or a placement.

## Spec (Auto Filter, 100 %)
- Separate 24 × 13 buttons with a 3 px gap; not joined into one bar.
- Selected: `orange` fill. Other: `button-off` fill with a `button-border` border.
- Typical pairs: 12 | 24, Pre | Post, L/R | M/S, Hz | ♪, ms | ♪, Internal | MIDI.
- A unit pair (Hz | ♪) sits under or beside the dial it switches.

## When to use

- **Use** for two (sometimes three) short options side by side: 12 | 24, Pre | Post, Hz | ♪.
- **Don't** when options that change the whole picture: mode buttons in the display.
- **Live 12 uses it** 11 times: Shifter, Spectral Resonator, Spectral Time, Chorus-Ensemble, Limiter.
- **Max:** `live.tab`, appearance 0 Default; livemode 1; mode 0 Equal spaced; rows 1. Status: docs.
